Great Tree Village Privacy Policy

Developer: Kealon · Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Great Tree Village (package com.gzso.greattreevillage) collects, why, and who it is shared with. Great Tree Village is a single-player game. It has no account system and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or microphone.

1. Game progress stored on your device

Your town, residents, progress and settings are saved locally on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

2. Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device, for example when a building is ready. They are created on the device and no information is sent to us. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in your Android settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a bonus. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and language, and how you interact with ads.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time with Ad privacy in the game's settings. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized ads. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device).

4. What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not run our own servers that collect your data, and the game contains no analytics or tracking of its own beyond what the service above performs.

5. Children

Great Tree Village is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to its processing. Because the game keeps your data on your device, you can delete it directly as described above. For data held by Google, use the controls and contact details in Google's privacy policy. For anything else, contact us.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
